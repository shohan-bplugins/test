# Easy 3D Viewer — overview for the marketing team

A plain-English guide to what the app does, who it's for, and what we can
truthfully say about it. Written for people writing listings, ads, emails and
support replies — no technical knowledge assumed.

**App name:** Easy 3D Viewer
**Category:** Store design → Product display

---

## The one-line version

Let shoppers spin, zoom and inspect a product in 3D — and view it in their own
room in AR — right on the product page.

## The problem it solves

Photos show a product from a few fixed angles. Shoppers who can't see the back,
the underside or the true proportions hesitate, ask questions, or buy and return
it. A 3D model lets them look at the product the way they would in a shop:
turning it over in their hands.

## Who it's for

Merchants selling anything where **shape, scale or finish matters** and photos
struggle: furniture, footwear, jewellery, electronics, toys, packaging,
equipment, homeware. Also anyone who already has 3D files from manufacturing or
product design and isn't using them.

---

## What a merchant actually gets

**Interactive 3D on the product page.** The shopper drags to rotate, scrolls to
zoom, and the model responds instantly. Works on desktop and mobile.

**Augmented reality, with no app to install.** Shoppers can place the product in
their own space straight from the browser — on Android and on iPhone.

**3D anywhere, not just product pages.** A second block puts a model on a
homepage, a landing page or a blog post. There's also a copy-paste embed for
places a theme block can't reach.

**Full control over how it looks.** Starting camera angle, zoom, lighting,
background colour, whether it spins on its own, which on-screen buttons appear.
If the model has built-in animations, they can play.

**A live preview while you set it up.** Every change shows immediately in the
same viewer the customer will see. Nothing is guesswork, and nothing goes live
until it's saved.

**Setup that doesn't need a developer.** Upload a file, pick a product, add the
block to the theme. A checklist in the app walks through it.

## Things we must **not** say

These are the claims most likely to be made by accident. Each one is wrong.

**"Works with any theme."**
The drag-and-drop blocks need a modern Shopify theme (Online Store 2.0). Older
themes can still use the app through the copy-paste embed, but not the blocks.
Say *"works with modern Shopify themes"*, and mention the embed as the fallback.

**"Upload any 3D file."**
The app accepts **.glb** and **.gltf** files only, up to **20 MB** each. It does
not convert other formats, and it does not create 3D models — the merchant
brings their own.

**"Multiple models per product."**
One product carries one model, and a model belongs to one product. This is a
deliberate limit, not a bug.

**"AR works everywhere automatically."**
Android AR works from the .glb file directly. **iPhone AR needs a second file**
in Apple's own format (.usdz), which the merchant supplies. Without it, iPhone
users still get the interactive 3D viewer — just not the place-in-your-room step.

**"Boosts conversion by X%."**
We have no figures of our own. Don't invent or borrow one.

## Frequently asked, and the honest answer

**Do I need a 3D model already?**
Yes. The app displays models; it doesn't make them. Many merchants already have
files from their manufacturer or product designer.

**Will it slow my store down?**
The 3D file only loads on pages where a model is placed, and everything is
served from Shopify's own network.

**Does it work on phones?**
Yes — the viewer is built for touch, and AR is a phone feature first.

**What happens if I cancel?**
Models stay in the library. Anything over the free plan's limit stops showing on
the storefront until the merchant removes enough to fit or subscribes again.

**Can I try it first?**
Yes — the free plan has no time limit, and paid plans have a 7-day trial.
