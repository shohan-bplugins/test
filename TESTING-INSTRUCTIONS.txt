WHAT IT DOES
Easy 3D Viewer displays a .glb/.gltf model as an interactive 3D viewer on the storefront through a theme app block. Shoppers rotate, zoom, and on a phone place the product in their room with AR. A model is attached to a product via the custom.model_3d metafield, which the app creates for you.

MAIN FLOW (~3 MIN)
1. Install. The app opens on "Get started", a three-step checklist.
2. Step 1 - click "Create definitions". This creates two metafield definitions with storefront read access: custom.model_3d (File) and custom.viewer_3d_config (JSON). Nothing else on the store is touched. Verify in Settings > Custom data > Products, or on the app's Setup page.
3. Step 2 - click "Pick a product", choose any product, drop the .glb on the upload area. The file goes to the store's own Shopify Files. A live preview appears with the viewer settings beside it; every change renders before you save.
4. Step 3 - click "Add block for me". The app inserts the "3D Viewer - Product" block into the published theme's product template. Or click "Open theme editor" and add it under Add block > Apps; the app detects either route.
5. Open that product on the storefront. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, try the controls: fullscreen, reset view, zoom, snapshot. No console errors.
6. On a phone, tap "View in your room" for AR.

ALSO WORTH SEEING
- 3D Model Library: manage models independently of products.
- "3D Viewer - Page" block: a viewer on any page, by Model ID or URL.
- Each model has a shareable /viewer/<id> link and an iframe embed - the path for themes without app-block support.
- A product with no model renders normally: no viewer, no error.
