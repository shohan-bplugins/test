Give every product variant (like Red or Large) its own price, stock and 3D look, let shoppers customize the product, and show it all on your store. Shoppers see the right 3D model as soon as they pick a variant.

✅ Before you start: the product needs a 3D model. In the app, go to Products, open your product and add a model under Model file.

1. Find the Product variants section

  1. Open Easy 3D Viewer in your Shopify admin.
  2. Click Products, then click your product.
  3. On the right, open the Product variants section ①.

The line under the heading ② shows how many variants you have and how many have their own 3D setup. The Live preview on the left ③ shows your changes instantly.

2. Add variants and options

Your product has no variants yet

  1. Click Add an option.
  2. Type an Option name, for example Color ①.
  3. Type the Values, separated by commas: Teal, Red, Black ②.
  4. Click Create option ③. One variant is created for each value.

Add one more variant

  1. At the bottom of the list, click Add variant.
  2. Enter a value for each option, for example Green ① and M ②. A new value is added to the option automatically.
  3. Enter a Price, or leave it empty to use the product price ③.
  4. Click Create variant ④.

Add another option (like Size)

  1. Click More ① → Add another option ②.
  2. Type the name (Size) and values (S, M, L).
  3. Click Create option. A variant is made for every combination (Red / S, Red / M…). A product can have up to 3 options.

Add or remove option values

  1. Click More → Manage options.
  2. To add values, click + next to the option ①, type them (XL, XXL) and click Add values.
  3. To remove a value, click its chip ②. To remove a whole option, click the delete icon ③.

⚠️ Careful: removing a value or option deletes the variants that use it from Shopify. This can't be undone.

3. Change price, SKU and stock

For one variant

  1. Click the ⋯ button on the variant ①.
  2. Click Edit price, SKU & stock ②.

A window opens. Each part saves on its own:

  • ③ Option values: rename this variant's values → Save option values.
  • ④ Price: price, compare-at price and SKU → Save price.
  • ⑤ Quantity: stock, or tick Unlimited quantity → Save quantity.

For many variants at once

  1. Click More → Set prices by option (or Set quantity by option).
  2. Pick which variants to change, for example Size = L ①. Leave everything on Any to change all of them.
  3. Enter the new price ②. Check Variants affected ③.
  4. Click Apply price ④. Repeat for other sizes or colours.

4. Preview a variant

  1. Click Preview on a variant ①.
  2. The Live preview shows that variant's 3D look, with a Previewing: … badge ②.
  3. Click Show product default ③ to go back.

5. Customize the 3D look of a variant

Step 1: Open the 3D editor

Click Customize 3D on the variant ②. The 3D editor opens for that variant only. A variant that already has its own 3D look shows a 3D setup badge and an Edit 3D setup button ①.

Step 2: Get to know the editor

  1. Variant: switch to another variant without closing the editor. Unsaved changes are kept.
  2. Badge: Same as default or Overrides default. Reset to default removes this variant's changes.
  3. Parts: every piece of your model.
  4. View tools: zoom in, zoom out, reset view, grid and auto-rotate. Drag the model to turn it.
  5. Settings: Material, Scene & lighting, Custom options, Pricing, Camera, AR and more.
  6. Top bar: Undo, Redo, Use view as variant image, Preview on store, Discard, Save draft and Publish.

Step 3: Change a part's colour

  1. Click a part in the Parts list, or click it on the model ①.
  2. Under Material, pick a Colour ②.
  3. Optional: adjust Metalness, Roughness and Clear coat for shine ③.

💡 Tip: changed something by mistake? Click Reset to the model's material, or Use default to go back to the default look for this part.

Step 4: Show, hide or name a part (optional)

  • ① Eye icon: hide the part for this variant.
  • ② Label shoppers see: a friendly name, like Cushion.
  • ③ Customer can recolor: shoppers get a colour picker for this part.
  • ④ Customer can hide: shoppers can switch this part off.

Step 5: Extra options (optional)

  • Use a different model: under Model for this selection, click Choose a different model. Click Use the product's model to undo.
  • Use the 3D view as the variant image: turn the model to a nice angle, then click Use view as variant image in the top bar. The picture becomes this variant's image in Shopify.
  • Edit the variant here: the Variant details panel ① lets you change option values, price and quantity without leaving the editor.

Step 6: Save and publish

  1. The badge ① shows Unsaved changes while you edit.
  2. Click Save draft ②. Nothing is live yet.
  3. Click Preview on store ③ to check it on your shop. Only you see the draft.
  4. Happy? Click Publish ④. The badge changes to Published.

6. More variant actions (⋯ menu)

Copy a 3D setup to other variants

  1. Click ⋯ → Copy 3D setup to…
  2. Tick the variants you want, or Select all ①.
  3. Click Copy setup ②.
  4. Open the 3D editor and click Publish to make it live.

💡 Copy 3D setup and Reset to default 3D only appear on variants that already have their own 3D setup.

7. Custom options for shoppers

Custom options let shoppers build their own look, like picking a leg finish or a fabric colour, and see it on the 3D model. You can charge extra for a choice.

Add a custom option

  1. On the product page, click Customize 3D on any variant to open the 3D editor. Custom options apply to the whole product, not just that variant.
  2. Open Custom options on the right and add an option. Pick its type: Color, Material, Parts or Material variant.
  3. Fill in the settings:
    • ① Option name: what shoppers see, like Legs.
    • ② Show as: Color swatches, Buttons or Dropdown.
    • ③ Parts this option changes: tick the parts.
    • ④ Default choice, and Shoppers must choose one if it's required.
    • ⑤ Show only when…: optional conditions, like "only when Color is Red".
  4. For each choice, set the ⑥ Choice name, its colour or material, and a ⑦ Price change: No change, Fixed amount or Percent of the price.
  5. Click Publish.

Check the price

Open Pricing to try choices and see the Total a shopper will pay.

See it as a shopper

Click Shopper view ① above the model to see the viewer exactly as shoppers do. Here you can also change Viewer style & controls and place Hotspots by clicking the model.

8. Show it on your store (theme blocks)

Add a block to your product page

  1. In Shopify, go to Online Store → Themes and click Customize.
  2. At the top, switch to Products → Default product.
  3. Click Add block (or Add section) → Apps, and pick one of the blocks below.
  4. Change its settings on the right, then click Save.

Product configurator block

A full product section in one block. When a shopper picks a variant, the 3D model changes right away.

  1. 3D gallery: shoppers turn and zoom the model.
  2. Thumbnails: switch between the 3D model and product photos.
  3. Variant picker: buttons or a dropdown.
  4. Customize: your custom options, with a live price total.
  5. Quantity, Add to cart and Buy now.
  6. Tabs: extra content such as Shipping and Returns.

3D product + Customize block

Keeps your theme's own product info and Add to cart. Choose a Display mode:

  • Replace the theme's product images: the 3D model takes the place of your product photos. Shoppers click the Customize button ① to open a side panel ② with variants and custom options.
  • Keep images, open 3D in a modal: your photos stay. A button on the photo opens the 3D model and options in a pop-up.

💡 Products without a 3D model keep your theme's normal images, so it's safe to add the block to every product.

9. Embed with iframe (themes without app blocks)

Older (vintage) themes, page builders and custom pages can't add app blocks. Paste a small code instead. It shows the full Product configurator.

  1. On the product page in the app, open Embed with iframe.
  2. Choose the ① Layout (default media, gallery position and height, 3D background), ② Width and ③ Product details (variant picker, what happens after Add to cart, vendor, SKU, quantity, Buy now, description). Click Save in the save bar.
  3. Copy the code:
    • ④ This product: for one product. Click Copy code ⑤.
    • ⑥ Product template (every product): paste once in your product template to cover all products.
  4. Paste it in your theme's product template, a page, or a Custom Liquid section.

FAQ

I don't see the Customize 3D button.

The product needs a 3D model first. Add a model on the product page, then the button appears on each variant.

My changes don't show on my store.

Save draft doesn't go live. Open the 3D editor and click Publish. Also check that a block from section 8 is added to your product page.

How do I undo a variant's 3D changes?

Click ⋯ → Reset to default 3D. Or in the editor, click Reset to default.

Do variant changes here also change Shopify?

Yes. Variants, prices, SKUs, stock and deleted variants are saved straight to your Shopify product.

I can't delete a variant.

A product needs at least one variant, so the last one can't be deleted.

Which block should I use?

Use Product configurator for a ready-made product section. Use 3D product + Customize if you like your theme's product layout. If your theme can't add app blocks, use Embed with iframe.
