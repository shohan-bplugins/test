# Plans, limits & billing

What each plan includes, exactly how models are counted against your limit, upgrading through Shopify's approval flow, and what happens when you cancel.

### 01 · Title — `0:00`

Let's deal with plans, because the one thing worth understanding properly is how models get counted.

### 02 · Pricing page — `0:07`

Open Pricing from the nav. Three plans. Free, Pro, and Pro Plus, with your current one marked at the top and highlighted in the grid.

### 03 · Free plan — `0:16`

Free is a real plan, not a crippled trial. Five models, both theme blocks, every viewer customisation option, and the live preview in the admin. For a store with a handful of hero products, that may be all you ever need.

### 04 · Pro — `0:30`

Pro is nine ninety-nine a month. It raises the limit to fifty models and adds the two settings that make a real difference to presentation — HDR skybox and environment maps, and material variants — plus priority email support. There's a seven-day free trial, so you can test the limit before paying anything.

### 05 · Pro Plus — `0:49`

Pro Plus is twenty-nine ninety-nine, and the model limit comes off entirely — unlimited. It's the plan for a full catalogue in 3D rather than a few showpieces, and it also has the seven-day trial.

### 06 · How counting works — `1:01`

Now the important part. The limit counts distinct model files, not places a model is used. If the same glb is attached to a product and also sits in your library, that is one slot, not two. It's the file that counts.

### 07 · Where the count shows — `1:16`

You can see the count on the dashboard, on the products screen, and here in the library — always as models in use over your limit, so there's no arithmetic to do.

### 08 · Hitting the limit — `1:26`

When you hit it, uploads are blocked rather than silently failing. The banner shows the breakdown — library models, product assignments, and the reminder that overlap counts once — plus a direct upgrade button.

### 09 · Limit error on a product — `1:39`

You'll see the same message if you try to assign a model to a new product. Note that replacing the model on a product that already has one is always allowed — you're not adding a file, you're swapping one.

### 10 · Upgrading — `1:50`

To upgrade, click Upgrade on the plan you want. The app hands you to Shopify's own billing.

### 11 · Shopify approval — `1:57`

This screen is Shopify's, not ours. It shows the price, the trial length and the billing interval, and the charge appears on your normal Shopify invoice. We never see your card. Approve, and you come straight back to the app.

### 12 · Active plan — `2:10`

Back in the app, the new plan is marked Active, the Most Popular badge steps aside, and your limit is raised immediately.

### 13 · Plan tile — `2:18`

The dashboard plan tile now shows Pro, your monthly price, the subscription status and any remaining trial days, with a Manage plan button.

### 14 · Cancelling — `2:27`

Cancelling is one button at the top, and here's the part worth knowing: it drops you to Free, but it does not remove anything. Your existing model assignments keep working on the storefront. Only new uploads beyond the Free limit are blocked.

### 15 · Downgrade safety — `2:41`

That's a deliberate design choice. A downgrade can never break a live product page, so you can move between plans seasonally without any risk to what's already published.
