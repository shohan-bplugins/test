# Overview & Dashboard

What the 3D Viewer app does, the three-step path to a live 3D product, and how to read every panel on the dashboard.

Runtime 2:27 · 13 scenes.

---

### 01 · Title — `0:00`

Welcome to 3D Viewer. This app puts real, interactive 3D models on your Shopify storefront — no code, no separate hosting, and no theme editing beyond one block.

### 02 · The payoff — `0:12`

This is the end result. A shopper lands on the product page and can drag the model to spin it, zoom in on the stitching, and open it fullscreen. On a phone they can even place it in their own room with augmented reality.

### 03 · Title — `0:24`

Let's start where the app opens: the dashboard.

### 04 · Dashboard arrival — `0:28`

When you open 3D Viewer from your Shopify admin, this is the home screen. Your store name sits under the title, and your current plan is in the top right.

### 05 · App nav — `0:37`

This row is the whole app. Product 3D Models attaches a model to a product. 3D Model Library holds standalone models you can drop on any page. Documentation, Pricing and Setup do what they say.

### 06 · Getting started checklist — `0:50`

If you're new, the Getting started card is your route. Three steps: create the metafield definitions, upload your first model, then add the theme block. Each one ticks green on its own once it's genuinely done — the app checks your store rather than remembering that you clicked something.

### 07 · Stat tiles — `1:06`

Four tiles summarise where you stand. Products with a model shows your coverage across the catalogue. Models in use counts against your plan limit. Uploads this month is your recent activity, and the last tile is your plan with a shortcut to billing.

### 08 · Models in use detail — `1:20`

That second tile is worth a closer look. It counts distinct model files, not assignments. So if the same .glb is attached to a product and also sits in your library, it uses one slot, not two.

### 09 · Recent models and activity — `1:34`

Scrolling down, Recently added models lists your latest uploads with size and date — click any row to jump straight into that model. Underneath, Upload activity charts what you've added, and you can switch it between day, week and month.

### 10 · Quick links and file types — `1:48`

On the right, Quick links jump to the other sections, and File types breaks down the formats in your library — handy for spotting a stray .gltf when you meant to upload .glb.

### 11 · Help and resources — `1:59`

At the very bottom, Help and resources gives you the video tutorial, the built-in documentation, and a direct line to support.

### 12 · The mental model — `2:07`

Here's the whole app in four steps. Setup once, upload a model, add the block once, then tune the viewer as much as you like. Everything else in this series is detail on one of those four.

### 13 · Title — `2:19`

That's the dashboard. Next up, the Setup page — the one screen you have to visit before anything reaches your storefront.
