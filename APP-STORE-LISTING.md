## Detailed description

**Turn your product photos into something shoppers can pick up.**

Photos show a product from a handful of fixed angles. Shoppers who cannot see
the back, the underside or the true proportions hesitate, ask questions, or buy
and send it back.

Easy 3D Viewer puts your product on the page in interactive 3D. Shoppers drag to turn it
over, scroll to zoom in on the finish, and — on both iPhone and Android — place
it in their own room to check the size before they buy. There is nothing for
them to install.

**Upload a model, place it, done.**

Upload a .glb or .gltf file, assign it to a product, and add the viewer block to
your theme. A guided checklist walks through each step, and the app adds the
block to your product template for you. No code, no theme editing.

**Control exactly how it looks.**

Set the angle your product first appears at, its zoom, and how it is lit. Choose
a background colour, add studio lighting or your own environment image, and drop
a soft shadow beneath it. If your model has built-in animations, play them, loop
them, or let shoppers switch between them. Every change renders instantly in a
live preview showing exactly what your customers will see — nothing goes live
until you save it.

**Not only product pages.**

Place a viewer on a landing page, your homepage or a blog post using a Model ID.
For anywhere a theme block cannot reach, copy a ready-made embed. Every model
also gets a shareable link you can send in an email or a message.

**Built for your store, not bolted on.**

Your files are stored in your own Shopify Files and served from Shopify's
network, so your storefront stays fast. The viewer loads only on pages where you
have placed a model.

## Accuracy guardrails

Do not write these — each is wrong, and each becomes a support ticket:

- **"Works with any theme"** — the blocks need a modern theme (Online Store 2.0).
  Older themes use the copy-paste embed instead.
- **"Upload any 3D file"** — .glb and .gltf only, up to 20 MB. The app displays
  models; it does not create or convert them.
- **"Multiple models per product"** — one model per product, by design.
- **"AR works automatically everywhere"** — iPhone AR needs a separate .usdz file
  the merchant supplies. Without it, iPhone shoppers still get the 3D viewer.
- **Any conversion-rate figure** — we have none of our own.
